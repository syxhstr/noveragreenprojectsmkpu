MinimallyFolium

Nature's Paper, Reborn.

MinimallyFoliumTech is an environmental initiative dedicated to reducing the carbon footprint of open leaf burning through the innovation of premium bio-stationery.

The Problem

Open burning of dry leaves contributes to 15% of suburban air pollution. We provide an eco-friendly alternative.

Our Solution

Using mango and Ketapang leaves, we extract pure cellulose to create textured, high-quality artisanal paper.

Your Impact

For every donation of 10kg leaves, you offset the equivalent of 4kg of CO2 from being released into our air.
