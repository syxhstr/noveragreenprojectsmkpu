Turning wasted leaves into premium eco paper — because nature deserves a second life 🌿

🌱 Our Mission

We transform fallen leaves and organic waste into usable, high-quality paper. Instead of burning or dumping natural waste, we turn it into something valuable.

⚠️ The Problem

Every year, tons of leaves are burned or thrown away, causing air pollution and wasting natural resources. Most communities don’t have a proper system to reuse organic waste.

💡 Our Solution

We collect leaves, process them into pulp, and turn them into eco-friendly paper products. Simple idea — big environmental impact.

🌍 Why It Matters

  • Reduces burning of waste
  • Improves air quality
  • Promotes recycling culture
  • Creates sustainable materials
