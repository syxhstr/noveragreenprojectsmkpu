Simple steps.
Amazing results.

We don't use harsh chemicals. We just use nature to help nature. Here is how we turn a dry leaf into a premium notebook.

1. Collecting

We gather dry leaves from local gardens that would otherwise be burnt as trash.

2. Softening

We cook the leaves in a natural solution to separate the strong fibers inside.

3. Pressing

The fibers are spread out and pressed into thin, textured sheets of paper.

4. Sun Drying

We let the sun do the work. No big machines, just fresh air and natural heat.
