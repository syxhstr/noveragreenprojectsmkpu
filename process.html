The Technology

Engineering
Sustainability.

How we transform raw organic matter into high-fidelity stationery through 4 precise stages.

SCROLL

01. Collection

We source dry biomass from suburban areas, preventing the release of harmful particulate matter caused by open burning.

02. Fiber Extraction

Using a zero-chemical alkaline bath, we isolate pure cellulose fibers from the leaf structure.

03. Refinement

Fibers are hydrated and pressed under high pressure to create a smooth, durable finish.

04. Sun Curing

Our sheets are dried using natural solar thermal energy, reducing our electricity consumption to nearly zero.
