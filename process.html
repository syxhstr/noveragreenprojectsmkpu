The Workflow

From earth,
to your hand.

We use a low-impact, high-refinement process to turn organic waste into professional-grade materials.

Step 01: Sorting

We hand-select only the best fallen leaves, ensuring the fiber strength is optimal for paper production.

Step 02: Pulping

The leaves are softened in a natural, chemical-free bath to extract pure cellulose fibers.

Step 03: Pressing

Fibers are hand-pressed into thin, durable sheets with a unique natural texture.
