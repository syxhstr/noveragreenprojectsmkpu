How We Make It.

From the ground to the gallery.

01. Collection

Volunteers collect fallen leaves (Mango, Ketapang, etc.) that are free from plastic waste.

02. Eco-Boiling

Leaves are boiled with organic soda ash to break down the tough lignin fibers into soft pulp.

03. Pulping

The fibers are refined and blended to create a consistent slurry ready for molding.

04. Pressing

The pulp is spread on screens and sun-dried, creating a paper with a beautiful natural grain.
