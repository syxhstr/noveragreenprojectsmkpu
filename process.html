Our Scientific Journey.

How we turn nature's waste into premium fiber.

01. Sorting

Leaves are manually sorted to ensure only high-cellulose materials like Mango and Ketapang are used.

02. Bio-Boil

A 4-hour extraction process using soda ash to isolate the pure fibers without toxic bleaching.

03. Drying

The pulp is pressed and sun-dried to create a high-strength organic paper sheet.
