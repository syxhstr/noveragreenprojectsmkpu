Scientific Methodology

How MinimallyFoliumTech converts biomass into circular luxury.

01. Fiber Selection

We specifically harvest senescent leaves (dry leaves) from Mangifera indica and Terminalia catappa for their high cellulose-to-lignin ratio.

02. Alkaline Digestion

Leaves are boiled in a mild sodium carbonate solution to remove non-cellulosic components, creating a sustainable bio-pulp.

03. Sheet Formation

The refined pulp is hand-pressed using traditional frames and sun-dried to maintain structural integrity and natural texture.

Why no bleach?

Most paper brands use chlorine. We preserve the natural 'earth-tone' of the leaves to eliminate toxic wastewater altogether.
